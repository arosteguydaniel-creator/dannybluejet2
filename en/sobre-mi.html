About me

Danny Blue Jet is the artist name of Daniel Arosteguy, a Chilean board game designer, musician and teacher.

He founded the independent publisher La Vaca del Tablero, through which he self-published Armaduras Musicales, a game for learning music theory, and edited Batalla de Coronas. He designed De Cero a CEO, a game created for the business administration program at Universidad de Los Lagos.

In 2026 he makes his international debut with My Extreme Skatepark, co-designed with Japanese designer Naotaka Shimamoto and published by itten, premiering at SPIEL Essen 2026.

He runs a school game library, where he uses play as a learning tool, and writes and performs his own music.
