Danny Blue Jet 🗻

Danny Blue Jet (Daniel Arosteguy) is a Chilean board game designer and musician. He co-designed My Extreme Skatepark with Naotaka Shimamoto, published by itten, and founded the publisher La Vaca del Tablero.
