A board game about the world of skateboarding, created by two designers from Chile and Japan.

About the game

My Extreme Skatepark is my first internationally published game. I co-designed it with Naotaka Shimamoto, Japanese game designer and head of itten, the publisher behind it.

It is a collaboration between two designers on opposite sides of the Pacific, brought together by board games and skate culture.

SPIEL Essen 2026

The game premieres at SPIEL Essen 2026 (Essen, Germany), the world's largest board game fair. Look for it with itten.
